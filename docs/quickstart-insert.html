TIDESY Quick Start Guide
1 Choose the Right Spot
✓ At least 3 feet (1 meter) from your router (the box with blinking lights from your internet provider)
✓ Away from microwaves and baby monitors
✗ NOT directly next to your router — too close causes connection problems!
2 Plug In
Connect the USB-C cable to a phone charger or USB port.
3 Connect to Tidesy
On your phone, go to WiFi settings and connect to:
Network: TidesySetup
Password: tide1234
4 Open the Setup Page
Open your browser and go to: 192.168.4.1
Be patient — may take a moment.
5 Pick Your WiFi
Select your home WiFi network, enter the password, tap Verify, then Save.
NEED HELP?
  • Can’t find TidesySetup? Turn WiFi off and on again on your phone.
  • Display says “WiFi not connected”? Move Tidesy further from your router.
  • More help: tidesy.com/help
